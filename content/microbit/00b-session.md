A 2-hour hands-on activity. By the end, every student will have built a working robot and programmed it themselves to follow a line and drive around an obstacle.

More importantly, they'll understand the idea underneath it: a robot senses its surroundings, decides what to do, and acts. This is the same sense → decide → act loop that runs in every autonomous machine.
